# Nested panel anchoring and row seams

## Confirmed symptoms and owners

User settings can shift the entire panel while opening nested sections; clipped descendants also inflate ancestor scroll ranges. The old header-pin shift in bevy_panel_runtime.js competed with the canonical layout/reveal owners. An asynchronous section could outlive the global toggle anchor. Persistent pinning also overrode deliberate scroll after later projections; closing a subsection returned to its parent. Keyboard contraction lacked a field-scoped scroll snapshot.

Hierarchy marks painted outside a scroll viewport remained fixed while their rows moved. Their terminal horizontal caps lay on the painted row edge, so the beginner List Play case covered the cap. The shared fixed-row chevron also centred itself from column width instead of row height.

## Durable correction

Reuse the existing layout, scroll reveal and panel runtime. Measure clipped child content as its viewport; reveal ancestors inside out. Capture one opening request at dispatch before asynchronous work, with mounted generation and manual-gesture revision. Consume it only when the expanded content is projected. Cancel stale requests after another activation, manual scrolling, closing or unmounting. Closing and later geometry/content updates preserve offsets, with real-content clamping only. This supersedes the former persistent expanded-anchor/parent-restoration rule. Remove the physical panel translation rather than offsetting it again. A repaint alone must preserve deliberate scrolling.

Compose passive hierarchy marks inside scrolling content, inherit its clip and skip already-decorated subtrees. Carry edgeGapPx from existing row layout; place the terminal cap at the gap midpoint while the header return stays at its centre. Bound/centre fixed-row controls from the row height. Do not hide the rail with a local mask, alter a document's geometry, copy a renderer or special-case the Play icon.

## Reproduction and regression gates

Open Profile/User → Settings → Preferences → Visual and its nested sections. Open, close and reopen them; resize with the last nested section open and after closing it. The current open header must meet the useful bottom edge for up-opening and the top edge for down-opening, with no accumulated blank space or panel translation. Scroll repeatedly, and inspect rail caps between every pair of stacked rows. In beginner List, stack rows above the project band and verify that Play never covers the upper cap.

Persistent tests: tests/eve/panel_nested_scroll.test.mjs, tests/eve/bevy_panel_dark_skin_contract.test.mjs and tests/eve/bevy_panel_selectable_list_contract.test.mjs. The system_ui_base contracts verify the single base and its alternate-size projection. Web guest settings and List cap screenshots are recorded in root temp/user-list-validation; final close/resize pixel replay, authenticated User, left/right/mobile keyboard/orientation and Tauri/physical iOS still require acceptance. Executable success does not replace that acceptance.


## 2026-10-10 — Keyboard and explicit scroll priority

The shared scroll owner captures nested offsets and a gesture revision before native editor focus contracts the surface. After viewport settling, the existing editing owner identifies the active field; projection aligns its bottom above fixed actions with the existing gap. Typing, paste, caret blink and ordinary refresh do not re-align. A manual gesture wins until field change/new keyboard opening; dismissal restores only if no manual gesture occurred. Rotation discards the old geometry snapshot, unmount invalidates pending requests. Render preparation occurs after asynchronous hydration/module/font work and before native/overlay paint, without extra padding or intermediate reveal frames.

Retained offscreen caret records are hidden by full projection. Opacity-only blink patches must also use current scrolled hit geometry: otherwise the blink revives the zero-opacity record whose disjoint nested clip is null. The existing motion owner now preserves this hidden state while keeping the source caret opacity available for its return into view.

Automated gates: bevy_panel_scroll_intent, panel_nested_scroll, bevy_ui_rounded_overflow, shared editor retention/Mystic, Contact, accordion, resize, manifest and existing rendering/reuse guards.
